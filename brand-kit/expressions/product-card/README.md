# Mez Systems Product Card 02

Product Card 02 is one programme with two deliberately sequenced phases.

## Phase A — Visual architecture, approved 1.0.0

Round 09 produced 14 keeps, 18 revisions and four kills. Round 10 then received 32 keeps, zero revisions, zero kills and zero unreviewed specimens. The exact human receipts are preserved in `round-09-feedback.json` and `round-10-feedback.json`. `H-EXP-04A-CARD-VISUAL-DIRECTION` is approved and Phase A is closed.

- **Landing plates** — 14 survivors. Every plate leads with the canonical public product name, follows with the canonical extended system name and uses a filled action. Wings grow only on requested treatments.
- **Product cards** — four survivors. Full-field and portrait anatomy use larger Wings, product-first hierarchy and filled actions; the continuous charcoal chassis remains.
- **System editions** — removed. MN01, MN02, HS01 and HS02 were killed in Round 09 and no longer render or appear in the review UI.
- **Bundles and packs** — six survivors. The two stacks, single card and two boxes remain. FN01 now uses smaller cards, even separation and a contained spread that does not collide with copy.
- **Website sections** — eight survivors. Kept shelves, explainer and offers remain; product heroes now use larger Wings, product-first hierarchy and filled actions.

The workbench contains 32 approved specimens across four active families. Every study presents an exact static version beside an automatically animated version. The dominant visible animated pane receives the one live Deep Mineral No. 5 core; the previously active pane returns to its exact static twin. There is no animation button. Repeated family and bundle objects remain static, the Wings and layout never move, and reduced-motion or renderer failure preserves the same composition without layout shift.

All card chassis keep canonical rounded geometry. Flat lower material corners occur only where the material joins a footer inside a still-rounded card, as requested. Customer-facing product indices, slash labels, internal gradient IDs, decorative archive numbers and forced disc or sphere objects remain absent.

The workbench remains a permanent regression proof for the 32 approved specimens. The complete taste synthesis and negative design boundaries are recorded in `brand-kit/docs/PRODUCT-CARD-DESIGN-ETHOS.md`.

## Phase B — Website component system

Phase B is approved canonical 1.0.0. Phase A remains the visual grammar underneath the website components, not a second gallery of review items. The exact Round 03 receipt records 11 keeps, 35 revisions and one kill; Round 04 then records 46 keeps. All 32 Phase A sources remain approved; `B-FT04` and `B-FT06` remain killed. The 46 surviving functional specimens cover discovery, feature placements, pricing, checkout, bundle offers, upsells and mobile continuity with explicit Phase A lineage.

`H-EXP-04B-CARD-FUNCTIONAL-PROOF` and `DEC-PRODUCT-COMPONENT-SYSTEM-001` provide bounded authority for the approved component design and interaction contracts. This package does not create pricing authority, payment-provider logic, a golden homepage, Figma or consumer code.
