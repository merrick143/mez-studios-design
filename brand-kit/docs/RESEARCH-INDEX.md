# Research and decision evidence

## Active evidence

`brand-kit/research/phase-2/` preserves the complete research cycle used during migration, including:

- Notion, Linear, Stripe, ElevenLabs, and Ramp evidence;
- comparative matrices and synthesis;
- Taste Reverse dimensions and source-level review;
- typography, controls, product-family, hero, motion, and identity calibration records;
- homepage studies and the later authority reset.

Research files are evidence, not current authority. Approved decisions are summarized in `brand-kit/governance/decisions.json`.

## Invalid work remains invalid

The first forced-comparison instrument, the three generic direction routes, the four homepage foundation studies, and the first synthesized homepage were rejected or invalidated. They remain available so future agents understand what failed: rushed vibe-coded composition, excessive boxes, weak product objects, misuse of Wings, forced serif, misaligned cards, decorative gradient text, and decisions made from outputs below the required quality bar.

Do not average invalid rounds into current taste. Use the authority-reset records and later approved calibration decisions.
