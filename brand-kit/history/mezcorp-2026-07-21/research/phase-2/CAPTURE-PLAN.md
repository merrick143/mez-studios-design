# Phase 2 capture plan

Status: H1 approved, deep research in progress  
Prepared: 20 July 2026

## Approved deep-research sequence

Notion, Linear, Stripe, ElevenLabs, and Ramp all receive deep treatment.

Parallel research assignments may collect independent source evidence, but every source must use the same evidence format and pass through one comparative synthesis before any Mez hypothesis advances.

Capture representative desktop, laptop, and mobile pages. Review discovery selections before accepting them. Add safe menu or accordion states where useful. Each package must answer at least two ranked research questions and identify source signatures that cannot transfer.

The five-source focus is intentionally narrower than the original panel. The synthesis must explicitly flag gaps created by removing standalone design-system, cross-category, and dedicated anti-reference sources. Do not fill those gaps through unsupported assumptions.

## Standard web capture

- Desktop: 1440 px.
- Laptop: 1280 px.
- Mobile: 390 px.
- Reduced motion by default, with a separate public motion observation when necessary.
- Home plus two to four surfaces that add genuinely different evidence.
- Safe menu, tab, accordion, and non-destructive demo states only.
- Record redirects, shutdown notices, inaccessible pages, regional differences, and failed interactions.

## Manual adapters

- Email: source, type, dimensions, client assumptions, image dependency, dark-mode behaviour, fallback, hierarchy, CTA, and legal footer.
- Ads and social: platform, placement, dimensions, campaign objective, focal count, copy load, CTA, recurring device, and performance evidence if public.
- Reports and decks: page size, grid, navigation, density, chart/table behaviour, hierarchy, printing, and accessibility.
- Motion: source, trigger, duration, easing, sequence, state purpose, reduced-motion alternative, and uncertainty.
- Product UI screenshots: public source, crop, state, content sensitivity, literal claim supported, and unavailable interactions.

## Qualification gate per source

A package advances only when:

- selected pages represent distinct surface categories;
- evidence IDs and screenshots resolve;
- the package validates;
- observed facts and interpretations are separated;
- limitations are explicit;
- the sanitised findings export validates after being copied away from Taste Reverse;
- no raw source media appears in the Mez consumer export.

Insufficient evidence may support a tentative experiment. It cannot establish a Mez rule.
