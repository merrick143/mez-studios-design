# Mez Systems homepage foundation synthesis

Status: approved human evidence captured, synthesis ready for one coherent build  
Review record: `homepage-studio/homepage-foundation-review.json`  
Production authority: none  
Prepared: 20 July 2026

## Blunt verdict

None of the four studies is an approved Mez direction.

`Layered clarity` is the strongest starting structure, but its hero mechanism, visual language, ethos treatment, proof objects and commerce section are not good enough. `System ledger` contains useful restraint and alignment behaviour, but the review provides no affirmative module-level evidence. `Kinetic shelf` contains details worth studying, but its hero and unequal product-card hierarchy are rejected. `Soft infrastructure` is rejected as a direction.

The studies still produced a useful result: the next build now has specific exclusions and a much narrower visual thesis. It should not average the four studies.

## What the feedback says Mez should feel like

Mez should be minimalist and intentional without becoming simplistic. Sophistication comes from exact hierarchy, spacing, alignment, real evidence, controlled gradient material and persuasive product presentation. It must not come from adding more boxes, connectors, colours or decorative movement.

The homepage should feel designed as one editorial system, not assembled from generic landing-page components.

## Dominant grammar for the next build

Working description: `Precise editorial operating shelf`.

This is not a public direction name. It is an execution constraint:

1. a quiet, highly controlled editorial field;
2. one strong idea per section;
3. equal geometry for sibling products;
4. real logos, screenshots and operating artefacts instead of invented UI;
5. a product gradient contained inside a deliberate product or evidence region;
6. bento only where it communicates a real relationship;
7. commerce designed to create desire and comprehension, not merely list inclusions.

## Retain

### From Layered clarity

- The full-page information order is credible enough to refine.
- The idea of a bento proof field can survive if every cell has a specific job.
- A dedicated product-gradient region inside the proof composition is worth developing.
- The light, calm foundation is a better starting point than a dark-first or soft-stone direction.

### From System ledger

- Consistent alignment and restrained geometry.
- Exact labels and a sense that the system is durable.
- Tighter control of colour and fewer competing treatments.
- Use as a restraint donor, not as a complete visual direction.

### From the source research

- Notion's ability to make product families clear without making the page noisy.
- Ramp's product and commerce framing, where value and proof are tightly connected.
- ElevenLabs' confidence in giving a product object one expressive moment while keeping the surrounding page controlled.
- Mechanisms only. Do not copy source compositions, characters, objects or component signatures.

## Reject

- The interchangeable-tools and plug-in-layer hero diagram.
- Any hero that requires the reviewer to decode boxes, connector lines and multiple simultaneous claims.
- Gradient-filled headline words.
- Three text-colour treatments inside one headline.
- Unequal product-card sizes in one sibling product shelf.
- Staggered, rotated or jagged sibling cards.
- Decorative misalignment in proof sections.
- Generic grey placeholder dashboards presented as evidence.
- A bento grid where the cells exist only because bento is fashionable.
- The current AI OS feature treatment.
- Purchase cards that read like a component demo rather than a compelling product offer.
- Dark sections used as an automatic way to create drama.
- Rounded boxes as the default answer to every composition problem.

## Foundation constraints for the synthesis build

### Identity

- Use the canonical Mez wings asset. Never approximate it with CSS shapes.
- Default to free-floating wings for the parent identity.
- Use a container only for a deliberate app-icon, product or small-format treatment.
- Real third-party company logos appear in one consistent neutral grey and keep their native silhouette.

### Colour

- Start with a light neutral field and near-black text.
- Use one coherent neutral-grey system for rules, labels, third-party marks and secondary evidence.
- Keep gradients out of text.
- Gradients belong to product media, product packaging or one purposeful proof cell.
- Do not use a gradient to rescue an otherwise weak section.

### Typography

- Use one primary sans-serif system in the synthesis build.
- Do not force a serif accent.
- One headline should not require colour changes to create hierarchy.
- Create hierarchy through scale, weight, line breaks, measure and space.
- Typography remains a candidate until the coherent homepage is reviewed.

### Shape and radius

- Keep buttons rounded, but not pill-like unless the role requires a pill.
- Use moderate, consistent rounding.
- Sibling product cards share the same height, width, radius and internal grid.
- Do not rotate or vertically offset sibling cards.
- Reduce the number of nested rounded containers.

### Spacing and composition

- Use a visible alignment grid across headings, evidence and product modules.
- Prefer generous negative space around one strong object.
- Avoid simultaneous micro-cards, connector systems and chips.
- Every section needs a distinct composition, but all sections share the same underlying grid.
- Misalignment must communicate meaning. Decorative misalignment is prohibited.

### Product shelf

- All sibling products receive equal geometry.
- Product differentiation comes from name, job, copy, gradient material and evidence, not card size.
- Product cards should feel like one authored family.
- Coming-soon states must be quieter than the live AI OS without appearing unfinished.

### Proof and bento

- Use real or intentionally redacted operating artefacts.
- Every cell answers a specific question: what it is, what it changes, how it works, or what proves it.
- Reserve one cell for the relevant product gradient or product packaging.
- Avoid fake dashboards and generic skeleton screens.
- Use real third-party logos where tools are named.

### Commerce

- Rebuild from the buyer's decision, not from the existing card component.
- Connect the promise, what is received, evidence, price and CTA in one persuasive sequence.
- Study Notion and Ramp for information hierarchy and confidence, not morphology.
- Show why AI OS is worth buying before exposing a future bundle.
- A future bundle should not compete equally with the only product available now.

## Next build

Build one complete synthesis homepage using the approved Notion section map, but concentrate design effort on four high-risk modules first:

1. hero with one quiet, intentional product or evidence object;
2. ethos with simple type and real neutral third-party marks;
3. proof bento with meaningful cells and one product-gradient cell;
4. AI OS purchase story with a subordinate preview of the future product family.

Then complete the remaining homepage sections using the same grid, type, neutral palette, shape grammar and evidence rules.

## Recognition tests

Before the next human review:

- remove the logo and check whether the hierarchy and product treatment still feel authored;
- remove gradients and check whether spacing, alignment and shape still hold together;
- view the page as a grayscale thumbnail;
- compare all sibling cards as silhouettes;
- confirm no section can be mistaken for a generic bento or SaaS template;
- confirm real wings and real logos are used wherever identity is represented.

## Unresolved variables

- exact primary sans-serif family and weight behaviour;
- final neutral white, grey and ink values;
- exact button and card radii;
- whether the primary proof object is a bento, a screenshot composition or a hybrid;
- the product-roster and gradient-ownership conflict between Notion and `products.json`;
- final commerce layout once the available product evidence is assembled.

These variables should be resolved through the coherent homepage, then at most two production-quality controlled comparisons for any remaining ambiguity.
