## Decision required

Complete the 12 controlled pair decisions in the local calibration instrument and return the exported JSON record.

## What is being tested

The pairs test recognition resilience, expressive range, density, proof literalness, family and product distinction, motion semantics, visible human control, machine portability, compact endorsement, composition, evidence hierarchy, and channel-native compression.

They do not test whether a named source brand should be copied. All specimens are original Mez concepts built from principle-level research.

## Review rules

- Choose the result that feels more Mez, more premium, clearer, more ownable, and less generic.
- Use neither when both fail.
- Use low confidence when the difference is too weak or context-dependent.
- Add one short note when a particular behaviour matters.
- Reveal the research notes after choosing.
- Do not approve production tokens or components during this review.

## Stop gate

TR-5 remains open until all 12 pairs have a choice and low-confidence, neither, or contradictory responses have been isolated. TR-6 cannot treat an unresolved result as a design rule.

## Owner workload

One 30 to 45 minute review. Follow-up should include only flagged results, not the full study again.
