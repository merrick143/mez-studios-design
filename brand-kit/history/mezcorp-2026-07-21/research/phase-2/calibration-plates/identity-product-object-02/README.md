# Mez product expression atlas 02

Status: motion slice approved, remaining expressions deferred
Authority: research only
Study: `MEZ-IDENTITY-PRODUCT-OBJECT-02`

This revision answers all three edit decisions from calibration 01. It replaces one universal animated disc rule with a contextual expression system.

## Revision response

- Static is the default for repeated, compact and exported uses.
- Living is reserved for one earned focal object per viewport.
- Hover growth and hover speed-up are removed.
- Scale uses contextual bands from 24 pixels through immersive crops above 1000 pixels.
- The actual MZ-G13 source and canonical Wings generate six individual expressions.
- Stack, bundle and constellation studies test collection morphology without approving a product roster.

## Disposition

`DEC-MOTION-002` promotes the Living Core and static fallback allocation only. The former isolated
nine-decision gate is retired to reduce human burden and avoid approving morphology without a real
job. The remaining static expressions and collection studies stay research-only until they are
tested in product-family and commerce context.

This atlas remains useful evidence. It does not grant production authority to cards, pills,
stacks, bundles or constellation layouts.
