# Invalid homepage synthesis 01

Status: invalid research round  
Preference authority: none  
Production authority: none

The synthesis at `invalid-synthesis-01.html` is preserved for audit only. It was technically functional but failed as design research. All eight reviewed sections received `change`.

## Why it failed

1. The Mez wings were treated as decoration instead of following the known white-wings product treatment.
2. Generic containers, equal cards and ruled boxes became a substitute for art direction.
3. Micro labels, small explanatory copy and numbered overlays added visible design labour without improving meaning.
4. Reference findings were paraphrased into prose but did not condition the composition strongly enough.
5. Too many unresolved system decisions were bundled into a complete page, making the feedback noisy and expensive.
6. Responsive and technical QA were mistaken for evidence of design quality.

## Donor fragments

The proof-section premise and the relative simplicity of the bundle section may be studied again. Neither is approved, locked or ready to reuse without a new visual test.

## Replacement gate

Do not rebuild the homepage. Complete `MEZ-VISUAL-TRUTH-01`, then build three calibration plates one at a time:

1. identity and product object;
2. hero and first viewport;
3. product family and commerce.

The first homepage can begin only after those plates produce an approved expression grammar.
