# 09 · Governance

## Locked

- Brand architecture: Studios → Systems → products.
- Radius is scale (product = pill, holdco = medium, parent = square). This is the brand-tier radius.
- Gradients = products; everything else is monochrome. The gradient is used on products only, never a page/section background or a bundle container.
- **Surface (V1, locked 2026-07-12):** ONE surface system, not five. Page `#F8F8F8` → recessed grey panel `#F6F5F4` → card `#FFFFFF` → dark UI / buttons / bundles `#0D0D0D`. Primary text `#0D0D0D`, secondary `#2E2E2E`, muted `rgba(46,46,46,.70)` (raised from `.58` for WCAG AA on 2026-07-17), hairline `rgba(13,13,13,.08)`. Gradient is the only colour.
- **Component radius scale (V1):** chip 8 · tile 14 · card 20 · panel 28 · pill 999.
- **Spacing scale (V1):** 4px base, 8 steps: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64.
- **Component conventions (V1):** primary CTA = dark near-black pill "Get the AI OS" (no price); product pill = white pill + gradient disc glyph + name; product card = white, radius 20, hairline, soft shadow, gradient disc; trading card = full-bleed gradient nameplate (portrait 3:4, radius 7.5% width, white 16% inner hairline), top-left dark glass eyebrow chip "MEZ SYSTEMS", bottom-third lockup of white wings (32% width) above the name in white Semi Bold, no paper/sphere, no floating centred lockup; stack = fanned deck (offset 50px across, 38px down, newest on top); bundle = dark `#0D0D0D` container, never a gradient container. See [07-ui-components.md](07-ui-components.md).
- **Sizing ratios (locked 2026-07-12):** sphere wings = 50% of the orb diameter (optically centred, nudged ~2% up); orb in a product card = 50% of the card width; trading-card wings = 32% of the card width. See [05-product-system.md](05-product-system.md) and [`../design-system-export/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`](../design-system-export/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).
- **Type (locked 2026-07-10):** Inter (Notion-tuned), Bold headings with negative tracking + tight leading; Instrument Serif accent; IBM Plex Mono. See [`../design-system-export/typography.md`](../design-system-export/typography.md).
- The mark is the wings; approved variations in [04-the-mark.md](04-the-mark.md).
- The disc card is the default product unit; always the disc. The sphere is reserved for identity boards only; glow is retired.
- The trading-card / deck / bundle system for collectible contexts.
- The gradient naming convention (`MZ-G01` to `G53`, raw library frame `312:57`; `MZ-G01` to `G20` catalogued with confirmed hashes).

## Do / Don't (summary)

**Do**
- Keep surfaces `#F8F8F8`/`#FFFFFF`, text `#0D0D0D`/`#2E2E2E`.
- Give each product one gradient core; show it through a treatment.
- Use the disc on product cards, the deck for stacks.
- Let corner radius encode tier.
- Show the price at checkout and on pricing surfaces, always. AI OS is one-time USD $99, never subscription framing.

**Don't**
- Put a gradient on the holdco or a page background.
- Use pure white for surfaces (brand white is `#F8F8F8`).
- Show a price on an ad creative or the default CTA. There is no blanket price ban: it is a per-surface judgement, ads stay price-free by default.
- Use the abstract glyphs (M monogram, layers, cube, etc.) as the mark without a logged decision.
- Invent new greys; use Ink tints.
