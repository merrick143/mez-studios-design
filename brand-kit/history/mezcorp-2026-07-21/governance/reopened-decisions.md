# Reopened decisions

These rules are reopened for structured research and H0 to H3 approval. Reopening does not invalidate current production guidance. Until a replacement is approved, the existing rule remains the safe default.

| Decision ID | Current rule | Why it is reopened | Earliest replacement gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-TYPE-001 | Inter plus Instrument Serif | The pairing lacks a demonstrated cross-channel type system, fallback proof, and dense-product validation. | H2 |
| DEC-DISC-001 | Flat translucent gradient disc | The motif is easy to imitate, weak at small sizes, and risks becoming the entire identity. | H2 |
| DEC-LIGHT-MODE-001 | Light-only marketing surfaces | Product UI, email dark mode, presentations, and accessibility require a broader surface strategy. | H2 |
| DEC-RADIUS-001 | Small closed radius set | Current values are not tied to component anatomy, density, or channel behaviour. | H3 |
| DEC-MOTION-001 | One duration plus generic easing | A mature system needs motion roles, reduced-motion behaviour, hierarchy, and channel limits. | H3 |
| DEC-PAGE-ARCHETYPE-001 | Fixed marketing-page archetype | One archetype cannot govern product UI, campaigns, commerce, editorial, and lifecycle channels. | H4 |
| DEC-CTA-001 | Small locked CTA verb set | CTA language needs journey, channel, intent, and accessibility rules instead of a short phrase list. | H2 |
| DEC-PRODUCT-TEMPLATE-001 | Four products with owned properties | The model does not yet prove how future products, suites, sub-brands, or acquisitions inherit identity. | H2 |

## Reopening protocol

1. Keep the current rule labelled `current` in production outputs.
2. Label research alternatives `candidate`, never `approved`.
3. Evaluate candidates against agreed criteria and representative channels.
4. Record the human decision, rationale, rejected alternatives, and migration impact.
5. Change canonical data only after the listed gate passes.
6. Preserve the prior version and provide a consumer migration path.
