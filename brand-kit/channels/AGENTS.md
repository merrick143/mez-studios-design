# Channel-pack agent rules

Read `brand-kit/AGENT-GUIDE.md`, `brand-kit/docs/END-TO-END-ROADMAP.md` and the selected pack's `AGENTS.md` before changing a channel format.

- Inherit canonical foundations and expressions; never duplicate or contradict them.
- Keep format craft approval, public-use approval, pilot status, outcome validation and whole-channel certification separate.
- Require authentic proof, golden examples, at least three anti-examples, editable source, export settings, validation and receipts.
- Treat Figma and Notion as mirrors. The repository pack owns the format contract.
- Do not launch campaigns, publish media or spend budget without separate user authority.
