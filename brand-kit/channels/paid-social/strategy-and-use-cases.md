# Strategy and use cases

## Job to be done

Interrupt a buyer who is already considering a costly service or build category, then make a lower-risk product first step feel concrete by showing authentic product proof.

## Best used for

- Products with a simple, current price and a clearly larger alternative category.
- Offers where `start with this` is more truthful than `replace the service`.
- Real interfaces that remain legible when composed into the lower half of a 4:5 static.
- Retargeting or consideration-stage creative where the audience recognises the category.

## Do not use when

- The category-cost anchor is anecdotal, stale, universalised or not publicly supportable.
- The product price has hidden recurring costs that the headline omits.
- The screenshot is fabricated, unreadable, private or not approved for the intended public use.
- The product is being represented as equivalent to migration, training, bespoke implementation or ongoing service without evidence.
- The ad needs a direct testimonial, launch or retargeting grammar that this pack does not define.

## Attention hierarchy

At one second, the viewer should read the category cost and the lower product price. At three seconds, they should understand the product name, first-step relationship and real proof surface. Outcome cards are tertiary: they reinforce proof but never compete with the headline.

## Recognition without decoration

The format should remain recognisably Mez when the glow is neutral or the Wings are absent. Recognition must survive through Geist/Inter typography, disciplined spacing, charcoal surfaces, the real product proof, restrained grid and the MZ-G product expression where used.
