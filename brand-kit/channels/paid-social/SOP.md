# SOP · Static: Price-Anchored Product Proof

Version: `1.0.0`  
Status: `Active` for assisted craft production  
Format status: `CRAFT_APPROVED`  
Publishing authority: human preflight required

## Purpose

Create a truthful, editable 4:5 product static from the approved price-anchor grammar, using current evidence and authentic product proof without changing the canonical brand system or the locked R07 references.

## Trigger

Run when a human asks for a price-anchored static, a lower-cost first-step comparison or a new batch based on the approved AI OS R07 format.

## Required inputs

1. Product ID, public name, destination and intended audience.
2. Current product price, billing basis, primary source and verification date.
3. Attributable category-cost evidence, verification date and a scope note explaining what the comparison does and does not mean.
4. An authentic product screenshot or composition from the local library.
5. Three outcomes supported by the visible product or approved product copy.
6. Figma access and the approved source-frame route.

Stop if a price, evidence source, destination, screenshot provenance or public-use status is missing. Do not fill gaps with plausible copy or a recreated interface.

## Procedure

1. Read this pack, the local skill and the AI OS screenshot manifest.
2. Confirm the format version is `1.0.0` and note any requested deviation. A new ratio or composition family requires a new review, not a silent extension.
3. Verify the live product price and destination. Capture the source URL and verification date.
4. Verify the category-cost anchor from attributable current sources. Avoid universalising a range or implying feature equivalence.
5. Choose the copy route: champion for directness, alternate for syntactic distance or safe for the clearest sequencing boundary.
6. Select proof from `brand-kit/assets/product-screenshots/`. Prefer native proof. Use a device composition only when the device materially improves comprehension.
7. Inspect privacy at full resolution. For public output, create or select a derivative whose manifest state is `approved-public` for the intended campaign.
8. In Figma, duplicate one approved R07 frame into a new dated section. Never edit section `1407:936` in place.
9. Replace copy and proof while preserving the locked reading order, 1080 × 1350 canvas, Geist Medium headline, Inter supporting system, restrained grid, downward glow and three-card outcome count.
10. When using native proof, keep the crop legible and reduce corner rounding. When using a transparent device composition, size against visible subject bounds and use no background card, fill, stroke or proof-area shadow.
11. Use the real Notion mark or canonical AI OS disc in inline and floating-card identities. Never substitute generic glyph tiles.
12. Render the complete batch and every frame at 1080 × 1350. Inspect at full size and at approximately 360px preview width.
13. Export PNG 1x in sRGB and retain the editable Figma source.
14. Run the craft validator and record an output receipt.
15. Before publishing, complete the campaign-specific price, comparison, destination and screenshot approval checks. Publishing, platform upload and spend remain human-authorised actions.

## Quality gate

Pass only when the category and product prices read in one second; the first-step relationship is honest; the product name and real proof read within three seconds; no outcome card competes with the headline; the screenshot is authentic and legible; icons are real and aligned; and all five visual-review dimensions survive a 360px preview.

Fail if there is a fake interface, fake notification, stale or unattributed number, service-replacement implication, unreadable product proof, gradient text pill, generic floating-card icon, over-rounded device frame, decorative proof card, missing receipt or unrecorded public-use approval.

## Definition of done

- New editable Figma frame in a dated section
- 1080 × 1350 PNG export
- Contact sheet for multi-variant batches
- Craft validator passes
- Evidence and screenshot provenance recorded
- Public-preflight status explicitly recorded as approved or pending
- Output receipt saved

A craft-complete static is not automatically published, piloted or outcome validated.
