# Paid-social format agent rules

Read `brand-kit/AGENT-GUIDE.md`, this pack's `README.md`, `SOP.md`, `content-model.schema.json` and `approval.json` before changing the format.

- Treat this as one `CRAFT_APPROVED` format, not a certified paid-social system.
- Never edit the approved Figma R07 section in place. Duplicate an approved frame into a new dated section.
- Use real product proof from `brand-kit/assets/product-screenshots/`; never fabricate a Notion interface.
- Raw masters and `public-safe-candidate` files are not approved for public use.
- Revalidate the product price, destination and category-cost evidence on every publishing run.
- Preserve the honest `start with` or `first` relationship unless evidence supports a stronger statement.
- Use the real Notion mark from `brand-kit/assets/third-party-marks/marks/notion/` when Notion is named.
- Use Geist Medium for the two-line headline and Inter for supporting copy and cards.
- Preserve the 1080 × 1350 canvas, light grid, downward glow, proof-led lower half and three-card outcome sequence.
- Device compositions have no background card, fill, stroke or shadow around the proof area. Size against visible subject bounds.
- Record build and export receipts. A passing craft validator is not a publishing approval.
