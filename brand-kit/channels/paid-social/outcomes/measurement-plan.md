# Outcome measurement plan

Current outcome status: `CRAFT_APPROVED`. No performance claim has been made.

## Hypothesis

For a category-aware founder or operator, a verified category-cost anchor followed by a truthful lower-cost first step and real product proof will improve qualified product-page attention without reducing trust.

## Pilot design

Compare the selected price-anchored static against a product-proof control with the same audience, offer, placement, destination and spend allocation. Keep only the opening mechanism different. Use at least the champion R07-01 and the safer R07-03 when the category claim is eligible for testing.

## Record these variables

- Audience and exclusions
- Placement and aspect-ratio delivery
- Offer, product price and destination version
- Category evidence and verification date
- Copy variant and screenshot asset ID
- Spend, impressions, frequency and run dates
- Landing-page version and any conversion-path changes

## Measures

Primary: qualified landing-page view rate and purchase conversion rate. Secondary: outbound click-through rate, cost per qualified visit, cost per purchase and comment sentiment about credibility. Guardrails: hide or stop creative if it creates material confusion about service equivalence, pricing or what is included.

## Promotion rule

Move from `CRAFT_APPROVED` to `PILOT` only when a named campaign, budget, owner and measurement window are recorded. Move to `OUTCOME_VALIDATED` only after enough comparable exposure and a written interpretation that separates creative, offer, audience, traffic quality and landing-page effects.
