## 1 · Observed expression

What is literally on screen. Structure, proportion, hierarchy, what carries colour, what carries
weight. Measurements where they are legible, marked as approximate when taken from an image.

Be specific about proportion and relationship, because that is what transfers. "The dark half is
about 44% of the card width" is useful. "It looks premium" is not.

## 2 · Underlying mechanism

Why it works. Name the device, not the decoration. What would break if you removed each element?

The test of this section: someone who has never seen the screenshot should be able to explain why the
design succeeds after reading it.

## 3 · Transferable principle

The rule, stated free of this brand, this palette and this typeface. One or two sentences.

If the principle cannot survive being restated without the source's colours and fonts, it was not a
principle: go back to rung 2.
