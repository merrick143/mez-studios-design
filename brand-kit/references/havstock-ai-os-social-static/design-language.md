## 1 · Observed expression

Olli's correction is the strongest reading of the source: "the Havstock ad is a landing page
compressed into one frame. Your five are a brand slide blown up five times." The useful standard is
therefore the complete sales argument, not the dark palette or the presence of a screenshot.

The eye meets a small audience label first, then a two-line replacement claim occupying roughly the
top quarter of the creative. The claim carries almost all the weight. A short product sentence and
two proof notes make the promise concrete before a pale application surface rises from the bottom
half. Three floating notification cards turn the application screenshot into a visible commercial
outcome rather than background texture.

The creative is dense at the headline and deliberately sparse around the product sentence. Density
returns in the application screenshot. The source uses a near-black field and a faint square grid to
hold the composition together, with warm orange limited to the inline Claude mark, bullets and tick
marks. The unusual device is the compressed proof constellation: one large working surface plus
three detached outcome receipts creates the impression of a complete operating loop in one frame.

Olli's second correction isolates a smaller device inside that system: "do something like this for
ours, maybe notion logo." The cropped reference shows a folder-mark product label interrupting a
sentence instead of sitting above it as another badge. Its usefulness comes from syntax, not from the
blue folder or its dimensions. The label hugs its icon and name, and the sentence resumes immediately
after it, so the mechanism is named at the exact moment the claim becomes concrete.

Olli's R07 direction isolates the headline mechanism directly: "the example price anchored really
well we need to do same." The first number tells the viewer which expensive buying decision is being
challenged. The second number is not merely a price reveal; it collapses the perceived risk of trying
the smaller first step. The question mark and imperative make the contrast read as a decision, not a
pricing table.

Removing the headline loses the reason to stop. Removing the application surface loses product
truth. Removing all three notifications keeps the product visible but weakens the immediate payoff.
Removing the audience label changes little, which makes it expendable in the Mez translation.

## 2 · Underlying mechanism

The static works because it resolves a complete argument in one downward scan. It names an expensive
or frustrating alternative, presents the product as the replacement, then shows the working surface
and the result together. The headline earns attention, while the screenshot prevents the claim from
floating free of a real product.

The price anchor also defines the category before it defines the discount. `$5,000` tells the viewer
this is about a built operating system, not another decorative template. `$27` then makes trying the
product feel smaller than commissioning the alternative. For Mez, that mechanism is safe only when
both the category range and the current AI OS price are verified and the wording says `start with`
rather than claiming feature-for-feature equivalence.

The notifications are effective because they annotate the product rather than describe it. They
translate a dense interface into three benefits a viewer can understand at social-feed speed. The
device would fail if the cards were generic decoration, if the screenshot were fabricated or if the
benefits claimed outcomes the interface does not prove.

## 3 · Transferable principle

A product static can carry a complete replacement argument when one decisive claim leads directly
into one authentic working surface, and a small number of truthful annotations explain what the
viewer should notice. The proof object must dominate the lower field, while every annotation earns
its place by making the real surface easier to read.

A category-cost anchor is strongest when the expensive number names the familiar buying route, the
small number names the reversible first step, and the sentence preserves the difference between
them. The contrast should reduce decision friction without pretending a product and a bespoke
service are identical purchases.

## 5 · What not to take

Do not reproduce Havstock's exact "$5,000 Developer? Use this $27 Claude Skill" wording, the Claude
star inside the headline, the commercial content of the three receipt cards, or the warm orange
accent system. A verified `$5,000` category floor may be used only as a custom-build anchor, never as
a claim that every consultant charges that amount or that the AI OS replaces discovery, migration,
training, integrations or bespoke implementation.
Do not recreate Notion chrome as vector decoration, add fake payment or booking events, or claim a
monetary outcome the approved screenshots do not prove. The layered argument, screenshot bleed and
truthful outcome plane may transfer because those are the mechanism Olli explicitly asked to retain.
Do not copy the blue folder, `Builder OS` wording, exact pill dimensions or source stroke treatment;
only the content-hugging inline handoff is retained.
