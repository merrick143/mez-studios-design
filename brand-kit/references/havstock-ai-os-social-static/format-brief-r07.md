## Copy thesis

The reference works because the first price names the expensive buying category and the second price makes a smaller first step feel immediately available. R07 applies that mechanism without claiming that a `$99` product replaces discovery, migration, training, integrations or a bespoke implementation.

## Verified copy inputs

- [`mez.systems/ai-os`](https://mez.systems/ai-os) states a current `$99` one-time price with lifetime access and updates.
- [Iteratum](https://iteratum.com/pages/notion-consulting) lists full Notion workspace builds at `$2,500–$6,000`.
- [Notionalize](https://notionalize.com/blog/notion-consultant-cost) lists a custom Notion OS at `$5,000–$15,000`.
- The advertising comparison therefore uses `$5,000` only as a public category floor. It does not claim that every consultant charges that amount.
