## 1 · Observed expression

The four images show one operating system at different depths rather than four interchangeable
features.

- **Oliver's Command.** The dark Mez banner and large page title arrive first. The eye then splits
  between quick routes, active task views and the live content queue. It is the densest screen and
  its unusual strength is that unfinished, current work is visible at the same time as the system's
  navigation.
- **MEZ Backend.** A landscape cover and large title establish the system boundary. Three equal
  domain routes sit above one wide Knowledge OS region. The empty space makes the information
  architecture legible before any individual document matters.
- **Mez Studios Docs.** The database title and view controls arrive first, followed by six grouped
  document types. Almost the entire lower frame is empty. That emptiness proves structure: the
  categories are the argument, not a large amount of content.
- **The Ultimate AI Ad System.** The named system and orientation panel arrive first, then the four
  operating routes, then the weekly production, experiment and live-ad views. It is the clearest
  evidence that the underlying operating layer becomes a bounded product for one real workflow.

Across the set, browser and Notion chrome are quiet provenance. Covers and emoji carry local colour,
but the argument is carried by hierarchy, linked domains, live views and repeated operating nouns.
Deleting the page titles would remove identity. Deleting the database and task views would remove
proof of current use. Deleting the covers and emoji would lose personality but not the evidence.

## 2 · Underlying mechanism

The set works because it moves from breadth to depth to application. The Command surface proves one
place can coordinate current work. The Backend and Docs surfaces prove that coordination is fed by
structured business context. The Ad System proves the same operating logic is packaged around one
specialised workflow. The screenshots do not need fabricated metrics because their relationship is
the evidence: command, context, working system.

Authenticity comes from retaining recognisable working-state details and ordinary interface chrome.
A glossy device mock-up would weaken the claim by turning an operating receipt into marketing art.

## 3 · Transferable principle

Prove an internal operating claim with real surfaces at deliberately different levels of zoom. Let
one surface establish the whole system, one reveal the structured context beneath it, and one show
that context being used in a bounded workflow. Captions should name what each surface proves, not
describe what it looks like.

## 5 · What not to take

Do not recreate Notion's toolbar, tabs, emojis, covers, callouts or database controls as Mez UI. Do
not perspective-skew the screenshots, place them in floating browser mock-ups or add decorative
shadows. Do not crop away every working-state detail in pursuit of polish. Most importantly, do not
turn the screenshots into claims about revenue, speed or performance that the pixels do not prove.
