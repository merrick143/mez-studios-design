# Feedback discipline

How to run a design round with Olli without burning it.

This file exists because of a specific, expensive failure. It is the most repository-specific
document in `design-authority/` and it is derived entirely from recorded evidence.

---

## The case study: Golden Homepage GH-S02, 24 July 2026

One section. Four rebuild rounds in one evening. All four rejected.

| Round | What was built | The response |
|---|---|---|
| Brief | | "come up with five to ten completely different ways … we're just not moving in the right direction" |
| Lab 1 | 8 concepts: editorial thesis, accumulating stack, disconnected interfaces | "why do you keep making stuff that is so default? I thought we have an ethos and inspo, do way better" |
| Lab 2 | 6 concepts, heavy motion: 178-node point clouds, over-wound coils, flow fields | "these are all too much I want simple in mez systems design ethos" |
| Lab 3 | 3 concepts stripped to pure type and air, no diagram | "none of those have any visual???" |
| Lab 4 | 3 concepts, simple layout plus one thin visual | "no wtf are you building" |

### The diagnosis

Every correction was applied to the **wrong axis**.

"Too default" was read as *not enough*, so round 2 added. "Too much" was read as *too many
elements*, so round 3 removed. "No visual" was read as *put something back*, so round 4 added a
thin one. The quantity dial went up, down, up. It was never the quantity.

**"Default" is a craft complaint, not a quantity complaint.** It means the composition is what you
would get from any competent model given the brief: the shape is generic even though the tokens are
correct. The fix is a different *composition family* executed at a higher standard, not more
primitives and not fewer.

**"Too much" from Olli means unearned complexity, not element count.** A 178-node point cloud is not
rejected for having 178 nodes. It is rejected because complexity is standing in for an argument.

**"No visual" means no protagonist**, not no ornament. A section of pure type has nothing for the
material to be the protagonist *of*.

All four rounds shared one composition family and varied the decoration on it. That is why "we
completely restarted" was true four times and false four times.

### The second cause: fan-out amnesia

Rounds 1 and 2 were generated by parallel subagents each told to "ingest the design system". A fresh
agent re-deriving the ethos from scratch in a single pass produces the statistical average wearing
Mez tokens. That is the literal mechanism behind "so default".

**If you fan out, hand each agent the conclusions, not the source.** The approved invariants, the
rejection list, the composition family it must use, and the specific thing that makes this section
different from its neighbours. An agent that has to infer the taste will not infer it.

## The round protocol

### 1 · Ideation in words, before pixels

Present five or six ideas as **plain sentences**. No HTML, no lab page, no rendering.

Each idea is: the composition family, the protagonist, the argument the structure makes, and what
makes it different from the others. Two or three lines each.

Olli kills the wrong ones in under a minute. This costs one message and it is the difference between
discarding an idea and discarding an evening. He asked for exactly this on 24 July: *"let's maybe go
first deep into ideation of what you think first and come out with a bunch of different ideas. Don't
make anything yet."*

### 2 · Build only survivors, at full craft, few at a time

Two or three, not eight. Eight variants at 60% craft teaches nothing except that eight things were
rejected. Breadth without craft has been rejected at every scale it has been tried: fifty
Product Card specimens in round 05 did not help either.

### 3 · Show it rendered, at real sizes, with motion running

Desktop and mobile. Never present a static description of something that is supposed to be alive.

### 4 · Atomic, addressable feedback

Every specimen independently keep / revise / kill with a written note. Record it as
`round-NN-feedback.json` beside the work. **The four GH-S02 lab rounds have no feedback record at
all**: the rejections survive only in a session transcript, which is why this document had to be
reconstructed rather than read.

### 5 · Preserve the champion

Once something gets a keep, it is a champion. Vary one or two controlled dimensions around it. Do
not reset the visual language unless Olli explicitly asks for a reset. "Broad aesthetic resets after
a clear human signal appears" is on the rejection list.

## Reading the feedback correctly

| What he says | What it usually means | What it does **not** mean |
|---|---|---|
| "default", "generic", "AI slop" | Wrong composition family, or right family at low craft | Add more elements |
| "too much" | Complexity without an argument | Delete things until it is bare |
| "no visual" | No protagonist for the composition | Add a decorative graphic |
| "not our ethos" | Off the approved grammar: check the invariants and rejection list | Invent a new visual world |
| "restart from scratch" | Change the family | Redecorate the same structure |
| "close" | The direction is right, the execution is off | Start over |
| "make it better" | Craft: proportion, alignment, optical sizing, hierarchy | Add features |

**When you genuinely cannot tell which axis is wrong, ask: in one sentence, with two options.** One
clarifying question costs a message. A wrong guess costs a round, and four wrong guesses cost an
evening.

## Scope discipline

The point of this repository is design-system consistency. **An unrequested change that forks or
alters a shared treatment is a cardinal sin here**, worse than in an ordinary codebase.

When the request is scoped, change only that. Two recorded misses:

- Asked to centre card content, an agent also added a hero-only material-overlay darkening. Olli
  immediately noticed the cards "looked darker" and asked why. Reverted.
- A card outline was written as a bespoke `rgba(25,25,25,.42)` literal rather than a canonical
  token. He found it "harsh". Bind to `--mz-border-default` `#DADAD6` or `--mz-border-strong`
  `#8A8A84` instead of inventing a literal (canon `COL-07`).

If a scoped change surfaces a real secondary concern, **raise it as a question**. Do not fix it
unilaterally and do not bundle it in silently.

## References are ethos, never components

When Olli sends screenshots of sections he likes, and this is his preferred way of specifying taste
they are **not** a spec to reproduce. R17 landed because reference screenshots supplied the
missing taste direction, and it landed correctly because the polish was translated into Mez rather
than copied: the SaaS status-green became a monochrome badge, the ambient glow was dropped, the fake
AI logos were killed.

Run every influence through the abstraction ladder in
[`../references/README.md`](../references/README.md): observed expression → underlying mechanism →
transferable principle → original Mez expression. A study that stops at step one is a copy.

## The habit underneath all of it

Look at the render before forming any opinion, including your own about your own work. Then say what
you actually see, including when it is not good. A round that reports "this looks great" about
something that does not is worse than a round that fails honestly, because it spends Olli's review
attention to discover what the agent already could have.
