Status: implementation plan active; no golden-output authority

## Outcome

Build one complete Mez Systems homepage that explains the company, establishes the owned-operating-layer thesis, introduces AI OS as the available product, makes the future product family legible, proves the “built on ourselves first” claim, and leads to one primary route.

This is not another component gallery. It is the first composed golden experience and the first test of whether the approved foundations, expressions, components, motion and navigation work as one system.

The page must feel:

- calm before it becomes detailed;
- product-led rather than gradient-led;
- precise, rounded and deliberately composed;
- materially alive at one focal point at a time;
- credible because the claim and its evidence stay adjacent;
- premium through proportion and restraint rather than glow, glass or decoration;
- recognisably Mez even in monochrome.

## Narrative architecture

The copy establishes one belief shift:

> Better AI does not remove the need for an owned operating layer. The model can change; the company’s context, workflows, decisions and history should remain.

The page therefore moves through five decisions:

1. **Orient:** Mez Systems builds the operating systems AI-native businesses run on.
2. **Name the enemy:** accumulated intermediary tools create dependency without ownership.
3. **State the principle:** rent the intelligence; own the operating layer.
4. **Earn belief:** Mez Studios runs and breaks the systems before packaging them.
5. **Route action:** start with the AI OS today; understand the specialised systems coming next.

Testimonials reinforce the first product after mechanism and operating proof have already been shown. They do not substitute for either.

### Round 08 · Hero fan compaction

Human feedback recorded 2026-07-23 in `round-07-feedback.json`.

Olli reviewed only the Round 07 hero and asked for a tighter, more compact spread: the two left cards raised and pulled right, the two right cards raised and pulled left, less overall vertical spacing — "like I'm almost spreading a deck of cards."

Tuning this exposed a real geometric constraint: every hero card's identity anchors to its own bottom-left corner (a deliberate, consistent placement rule), but in a horizontally symmetric fan that placement is not symmetric relative to the overlap direction — a right-side card's label sits on the side its more-central neighbour overlaps into, while a left-side card's label sits on the safe side. Full legibility for every card therefore bounds how far the spread can compact.

Round 08 resolves this by scoping a smaller identity mark to the hero cards specifically (smaller Wings icon, tighter internal spacing) rather than the shared component used by the ecosystem shelf and final-route card, lowering the clearance each card needs. Combined with a tighter horizontal overlap and a shallower per-card vertical step, the deck compacts substantially — vertical drop cut by roughly 20 percent per step, the reserved height below the deck cut by roughly 40 percent — while every card's public name and extended name stay fully legible, verified per element by hit-testing rather than a visual scan alone. `round-07-feedback.json` records the constraint and the exact resolution.
