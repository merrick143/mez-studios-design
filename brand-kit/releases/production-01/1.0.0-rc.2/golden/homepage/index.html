Questions, answered

What operators usually ask first.

The short version before you explore the systems in detail.

01 What is a Mez System?

A focused operating system for one part of an AI-native business. It connects the context, workflows, decisions and history needed to do that work well.

02 What is the AI OS?

The Mez System available now: a structured operating layer inside Notion for tasks, projects, clients, goals, decisions, documentation and planning.

03 Do I have to replace ChatGPT, Claude or Notion AI?

No. Use the model or tool that fits the job. The operating layer keeps the business context underneath it, so the intelligence can change without resetting the company.

04 Why not use one AI product for everything?

Different parts of the business need different workflows, permissions and operating rules. Mez Systems stay focused while connecting to the same owned context layer.

05 Who is the AI OS for?

Founders and operators building AI-native businesses who want AI connected to real work, rather than isolated chats and tools.

06 What is coming next?

Context Engine, AI Ads System, Claude Code OS and Organic Content OS are in development as focused systems for the work AI-native businesses do.
