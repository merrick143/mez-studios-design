# Notion creative reference notes

Status: `reference-only`

These files are useful for studying how Notion presents real product detail without letting the interface collapse into an unreadable mockup.

Reusable observations:

- The product surface stays visually dominant. Campaign decoration supports it but does not replace it.
- Layered UI crops expose a few readable jobs rather than a full-page screenshot at illegible scale.
- Mobile and desktop frames are used selectively to establish context, not as decoration around every screenshot.
- Motion isolates one product action at a time and keeps a complete static frame.
- Dark campaign title cards are separate from light product proof rather than tinting the interface itself.

Do not copy the characters, campaign artwork, colour values, layouts or interface content. Source URLs and licensing are unverified, and nothing in this collection is Mez product proof.
