# TR-6 three-direction brief

Status: approved to prototype, not approved for production
Input: `REFERENCE-TASTE-SYNTHESIS.md`, `reference-taste-profile.json`, current Mez anchors
Human gate: holistic review of three complete cross-channel routes

## Shared brief

Design Mez Systems as a premium software and knowledge company whose products turn ambiguous work into visible, verified progress. The system must support websites, product UI, ads, emails, reports, decks, small-format identity, and motion without becoming eight unrelated brands or one repeated template.

Every route must use the same content and product facts so the review compares design direction rather than copywriting.

### Shared content

- Parent: Mez Systems
- Product: AI OS
- Core claim: Turn a request into verified work.
- Supporting claim: AI OS coordinates sources, decisions, production, and review while keeping the next human action visible.
- Demonstration task: Prepare the quarterly launch plan.
- Input: campaign brief, product data, prior results, approved claims
- Current state: evidence verified, one decision awaiting review
- Output: launch plan, channel variants, evidence record
- Owner: Olli
- Primary action: Review the decision

### Shared surfaces

1. Website hero and proof section
2. AI OS product interface state
3. Four-product family module
4. 1080 by 1350 advertisement
5. 600-pixel email
6. 16:9 report or deck page
7. Small-format parent and product identity
8. Four-frame motion storyboard with a reduced-motion equivalent

### Shared non-negotiables

- Current parent and product names
- Light-first Mez surface system
- Product gradients only on product-owned elements
- No glow, halo, corporate gradient background, or source signature
- Product is the protagonist
- Visible input, system action, output, verification, owner, and next human action where the surface permits
- Australian English, sentence case, no em dash or prose double hyphen
- Responsive and channel-native composition
- No production authority until Olli approves a direction and the later validation gates pass

## Direction A: Kinetic intelligence

### Thesis

Make advanced software feel alive, premium, and culturally current without sacrificing comprehension. Energy comes from controlled scale shifts, product-owned media, decisive crops, and semantic state movement.

### Why it exists

This route responds to the strongest ElevenLabs signals: energetic, intelligent, premium, product-led, and ownable. It also absorbs Linear's product proof and refuses Stripe's corporate gradient atmosphere.

### Visual system

- Large confident sans typography with one sharp editorial serif intervention
- Wide asymmetric compositions with one oversized product-owned object
- Cropped interface moments that reveal inputs and outputs rather than decorative dashboards
- Monochrome page chrome with the AI OS core used as a bounded signal, cursor, state marker, or media aperture
- High contrast between spacious narrative areas and concentrated working states
- Semantic motion that moves a task from request to evidence to human decision
- Small-format identity retains one bold product cue and a clear parent endorsement

### Emotional target

Energetic, premium, intelligent, ownable, product-first.

### Failure conditions

- Feels like creative-AI advertising
- Uses colour as atmosphere rather than product meaning
- Product proof becomes spectacle
- Motion communicates activity but not a state change
- Resembles ElevenLabs, Stripe, or another source in a logo-free thumbnail

### Expression refusals

- No waveforms, voice-player language, Chladni graphics, or media-brand motifs
- No orange, pink, and purple infrastructure field
- No scattered floating product cards
- No slogan-first hero with proof deferred far below

## Direction B: Evidence architecture

### Thesis

Make intelligence visible through structure. Mez becomes recognisable through an original evidence rhythm, routing geometry, verification edges, state labels, and the alternation of editorial calm with operational inspection.

### Why it exists

This route combines Notion's clarity and pacing with Linear's premium product evidence. It answers the demand for precision and intelligence while preventing restraint from becoming under-designed.

### Visual system

- Strong grotesk hierarchy with restrained serif annotations for consequence, judgement, and human voice
- A recurring sequence: request, sources, system action, evidence, decision, owner
- Original route lines and verification notches that show relationships rather than decorate panels
- Calm off-white narrative fields followed by bounded near-black operational sections
- Tables and dense states only when the receiver needs to inspect or act
- Product gradients appear only in state markers, product discs, or output certificates
- Small-format identity uses precise parent and product hierarchy with a recurring verification edge

### Emotional target

Precise, intelligent, credible, premium, calm but authored.

### Failure conditions

- Looks like Linear with lighter pages
- Becomes a dark developer-tool system
- Feels bureaucratic or compliance-led
- Routing geometry becomes ornamental circuitry
- Every channel repeats the same evidence table

### Expression refusals

- No black-everywhere theme
- No purple interface accents or source-like command styling
- No anonymous card grid
- No proof object without an explicit claim and receiver action

## Direction C: Human systems editorial

### Thesis

Present Mez as a deeply human company that happens to build powerful AI systems. Use editorial sequencing, people, decisions, and consequences to make software valuable without softening its intelligence.

### Why it exists

Human and cinematic signals were useful across Notion and Stripe, while the trust chapter was otherwise weak. This route invents a stronger proof model around accountable people, visible system work, and the moment a decision changes an outcome.

### Visual system

- Editorial serif leads the narrative, supported by disciplined sans UI and mono evidence captions
- Human moments are specific: reviewer, operator, customer decision, or artefact owner
- Photography or portrait crops appear only beside literal system evidence and attributed outcomes
- Off-white paper, black ink, strong rules, marginal annotations, and occasional full-bleed near-black chapters
- Product UI is framed as evidence inside a larger decision story
- Product gradients remain small, concentrated signs of system ownership
- Motion moves attention from human request to system work to returned human agency
- Small-format identity feels like a publishing imprint endorsed by a software parent

### Emotional target

Human, premium, editorial, credible, intelligent, culturally durable.

### Failure conditions

- Becomes a lifestyle magazine
- Uses stock photography as trust theatre
- Product disappears behind storytelling
- Feels quiet but not ownable
- Editorial typography damages operational clarity

### Expression refusals

- No generic customer quote cards
- No staged founder portrait as the whole proof
- No newspaper cosplay
- No serif applied to product controls or dense operational data

## Review questions

For each direction, Olli answers:

1. Would I be proud for Mez to look like this?
2. Does this feel premium, intelligent, clear, and product-led?
3. Does it feel ownable without resembling one source brand?
4. Which exact behaviours should survive if this route loses?
5. Which surfaces expose a weakness or contradiction?
6. Is the route a contender, useful donor, or reject?

The review permits one lead direction, one or more donors, or none. It does not force a winner when the work is not strong enough.
