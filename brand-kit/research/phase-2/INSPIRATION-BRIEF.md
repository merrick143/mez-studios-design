# Phase 2 inspiration brief

Status: `H1_APPROVED`
Prepared: 20 July 2026
Owner: programme
Human gate: H1 reference priorities and anti-references

## Purpose

Find mechanisms that can help Mez become more ownable, credible, and flexible across channels without turning the identity into a collage of admired brands.

The work does not ask which brand Mez should resemble. It asks which design problems matter, how strong systems solve them, what principle transfers, and what an original Mez implementation could be.

## Ranked research questions

### Priority 1: recognition without the obvious devices

How can Mez remain recognisable when the wings, company name, product name, and gradient core are removed?

Success means identifying reusable monochrome composition, geometry, density, type, and motion behaviours that can be tested without product colour.

### Priority 2: one family without four reskins

How can AI OS, Aurora, Prism, and Forge feel related while owning visibly different proof styles, density, data behaviour, motion, and campaign territories?

Success means a product-family model that communicates shared chassis and distinct jobs rather than colour substitution.

### Priority 3: literal, credible product proof

How do excellent software brands connect a claim to a product mechanism, interface state, result, or quantified proof without decorative mockups or vague AI language?

Success means a repeatable claim-to-proof grammar for marketing, product UI, ads, email, and reports.

### Supporting questions

4. How should density and composition change between calm executive marketing, dense operations UI, small ads, email, and reports?
5. How should Mez Studios, Mez Systems, and product endorsements remain legible at small sizes and in free-floating contexts?
6. What motion behaviours communicate system state, intelligence, and orchestration without ornamental AI effects?
7. How do mature systems make approved decisions portable between code, Figma, templates, humans, and LLMs?
8. Which current AI and SaaS expressions should Mez explicitly reject, even when they are polished or popular?

## Research lanes

Competitive-convention research identifies table stakes, customer expectations, proof patterns, and category clichés. It does not supply the visual north star.

Inspiration research studies narrow mechanisms from craft exemplars, mature systems, cross-category brands, and channel specialists. It must translate through an original Mez hypothesis before influencing a production decision.

## First-cycle channel scope

Supported:

- Public marketing websites at desktop, laptop, and mobile widths.
- Public documentation and design-system sites.
- Public product screenshots, demos, changelogs, and case studies.
- Public email guidance and examples.
- Public reports, PDFs, and editorial surfaces where access and rights allow observation.
- Safe public interactions and reduced-motion comparisons where the collector supports them.

Unsupported or manual-only:

- Authenticated product interfaces.
- Private design libraries, analytics, experiments, or source code.
- Paid-media libraries requiring login or uncertain reuse rights.
- Complete email-client rendering and inbox placement.
- Motion hidden inside inaccessible video or proprietary applications.
- Source assets, marks, copy, screenshots, or exact token values in Mez deliverables.

## Evidence and rights boundary

Capture date is recorded per source. Only public rendered output is observed. Sources retain all rights in their work. Screenshots remain internal research evidence and are excluded from Mez distribution packs. Every inference carries confidence and evidence IDs. Exact source values remain in the evidence layer and never become Mez tokens automatically.

## Success criteria

- Five human-selected software sources receive deep, comparable study.
- Each source answers a named question and has an explicit anti-copy boundary.
- The comparative synthesis produces at least three cross-source mechanisms for each priority question.
- Category conventions and inspiration mechanisms remain visibly separated.
- Every proposed Mez translation combines multiple inputs or derives directly from a Mez requirement.
- The final H2 directions cannot be described as another brand with Mez gradients.
- Missing standalone design-system, cross-category, and dedicated anti-reference coverage remains visible as a limitation.

## Stop criteria

- Stop a claim when its evidence is too narrow, inaccessible, time-dependent, or contradictory.
- Stop a source when it adds no new mechanism to the panel.
- Stop any translation that copies a source signature, component composition, asset, or token combination.
- Stop promotion when a finding has no Mez problem, original translation, prototype, validation result, and human decision.
- Do not start the canonical token or component factory before H2 approves a north-star direction.
