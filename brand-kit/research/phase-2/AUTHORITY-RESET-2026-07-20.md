# Mez Systems authority reset

Date: 20 July 2026
Scope: brand and website research authority
Decision: stop homepage iteration and separate identity truth from generated interface habits

## Blunt verdict

The pack contains a strong identity seed, useful research and too much generated certainty. It has repeatedly promoted speculative UI grammar into design-system language before that grammar earned human approval. The result is internally documented but visually unstable. Another complete homepage would compound the problem.

## Authority classes

### Locked identity inputs

- The approved Mez gradient library and its provenance, including `MZ-G13` for AI OS.
- The canonical two-path Mez wings geometry.
- Parent-brand wings may appear as free-floating ink or white artwork.
- Product expression uses white wings directly inside the gradient product object. Do not place dark wings on a white inset tile inside the gradient.
- The product name `AI OS`.
- The approved homepage positioning, belief hierarchy and source rights.
- The current production site remains the default until a replacement passes the full gate.

Locked means safe input to exploration. It does not mean every existing rendering of that input is correct.

### Provisional and reopened

- Typography families, scale, tracking and line-height.
- Neutral palette, contrast hierarchy and background sequence.
- Corner radii, spacing rhythm and layout density.
- Button anatomy and interaction language.
- Product objects, cards, shelves, stacks and commerce morphology.
- Section archetypes, page layouts and responsive composition.
- Screenshot frames, media behaviour, icon treatment and motion.
- Product-family roster and naming beyond the separately locked `AI OS` name.
- Generated Figma boards and brand-system documents that describe these choices as settled.

These are hypotheses. They require calibrated visual evidence and human approval before promotion.

### Rejected research patterns

- The four homepage foundation studies as design directions.
- `MEZ-HOMEPAGE-SYNTHESIS-01` as a coherent expression system.
- The phrase and concept `precise editorial operating shelf` as an approved direction.
- Generic box-first composition, ruled ledgers, bento by default and equal-card repetition.
- Numbered overlays, micro-label decoration and excessive explanatory copy.
- Tool-logo rows inside the hero when integrations need their own chapter.
- White inset tiles with dark wings inside gradient product objects.
- Generic testimonial grids and generic portfolio openings.
- Full-page builds before typography, radius, spacing, product object and section rhythm are calibrated.

Rejected means do not use as a starting point. A narrow fragment can return only through a new, explicit test.

## Corrected order of work

1. Complete a visual truth board using exact reviewed source evidence.
2. Human locks, edits or rejects six mechanisms and six refusals.
3. Build one identity and product-object calibration plate.
4. Build and review one hero and first-viewport calibration plate to expose the unresolved dependencies.
5. Calibrate typography as a complete cross-channel role system.
6. Calibrate buttons and controls as a complete semantic and interaction system.
7. Calibrate product-family and commerce composition in context.
8. Rebuild the hero as a centred, mobile-first, multi-product homepage opening.
9. Record an expression grammar from approved plates.
10. Build one golden homepage with the approved copy.
11. Validate originality, responsiveness, accessibility and conversion logic.
12. Extract tokens and components from the approved golden example.
13. Transfer approved primitives into Figma and the portable LLM pack.

## Human burden

The human reviews twelve evidence-backed statements now, then one plate at a time. Notes are optional unless a mechanism needs correction. No broad design-system proof session is required, and no complete page is presented until the underlying grammar is legible.
