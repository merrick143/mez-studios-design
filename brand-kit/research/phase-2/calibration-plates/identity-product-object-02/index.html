Revision after calibration 01

One core.
Two energy states.
Six expressions.

AI OS keeps one source material and one Wings geometry. Context determines whether it is still or living, compact or immersive, free or carried by a product surface.

New rule Motion is allocated by attention, not added through hover.

01 Motion allocation

Still by default.
Living when focal.

Both states use the same MZ-G13 source. Static is not a fallback or lesser version. It is the correct state when the object repeats, becomes small or supports a decision.

STATIC CORE

Recognition without demand.

  • Navigation and compact UI
  • Email, ads, reports and social exports
  • Repeated cards, bundles and comparison rows
  • Checkout, receipts and order summaries

LIVING CORE

Energy where attention is earned.

  • One primary hero object
  • Launch or product-reveal moments
  • Interactive selectors and demonstrations
  • At most one living focal object per viewport

No hover growth. No hover speed-up. The living material moves slowly at rest and keeps its physical scale.

02 Contextual scale

The composition sets the maximum.

Scale is expressed as working bands, not a fixed list of endpoints. The object can move below 72 pixels or beyond 440 when its role and crop remain legible.

03 Expression family

The same identity changes posture.

These are not six logos. They are six contextual expressions generated from one gradient record and one Wings geometry.

04 Collection readiness

A product can become a family without turning into a card grid.

The forms below test spatial grammar only. Repeated AI OS cards do not imply a product roster or approved bundle content.
