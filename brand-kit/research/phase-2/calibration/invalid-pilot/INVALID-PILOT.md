## Verdict

The round cannot support `HUMAN-TASTE-PROFILE.md`, a Mez direction, or any production decision. It tested reactions to low-quality, rushed renderings as much as it tested taste. The pair specifications remain research questions only.

## Evidence

- 12 decisions completed in approximately 157 seconds
- five neither responses
- one explicit low-confidence response
- confidence missing from nine responses
- no explanatory comments
- missing confidence was not flagged by the original contradiction logic
- several choices cannot be distinguished from a preference for the less weak rendering
- the reviewer explicitly reported that several designs were poor and that the instrument did not support confident input

The exact exported JSONL is preserved in `round-01-preference-log.jsonl` for audit history. It must not be merged into any valid preference dataset.

## Causes

1. Controlled concepts were rendered before a compelling Mez territory existed.
2. Both options were not consistently production-worthy.
3. The same generic miniature interface structure contaminated multiple dimensions.
4. Some visual implementations changed more than the named variable.
5. Several channel and motion concepts were symbolic approximations rather than credible examples.
6. Pair-specific research framing risked biasing the decision.
7. The flow did not require confidence or explain missing confidence as an uncertainty state.

## Replacement method

1. Mine taste from 35 high-quality real references inside Taste Reverse.
2. Capture love, useful, indifferent, reject, or not useful.
3. Capture up to three causal reason tags for meaningful positive and negative signals.
4. Synthesise the record into briefs for three original Mez directions.
5. Review complete cross-channel directions before running any further controlled comparison.
6. Use high-fidelity A/B tests only for the final four to six unresolved questions.
