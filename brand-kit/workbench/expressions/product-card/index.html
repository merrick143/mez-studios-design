Phase A · visual architecture

These are website cards.
That is the thing being reviewed.

Round 01 confused art direction with component design. Round 02 holds one real product-card anatomy steady, makes it narrower and more portrait-led, then compares four credible visual treatments in actual website placements.

WHAT CHANGED
  1. 4 candidates, not 10 experiments. Weak and unexplained concepts are gone.
  2. Real contexts. Every candidate is tested in a website shelf, feature placement and mobile width.
  3. One component system. Phase B will turn the selected language into website discovery, pricing, checkout and bundle components.

What you are deciding

One architecture.
Four treatments.

The choice is not “which poster looks coolest?” It is which treatment gives Mez a recognisable product-card language without losing clarity, then where the more expressive treatments are allowed.
