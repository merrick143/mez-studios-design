Everything except the final marker is identical. Same copy, same four charcoal dots, same line, same blend. The only variable is what the thing that ships is expressed as.

Shapes are the renderer's own. disc, sphere, rect and wings are the four masks mz-core ships; a pill and a rounded square are both rect at different corner radii. Nothing here is a shape drawn in CSS over a gradient.

One live core per section, blended live to live, and only the section you are looking at holds one.
