A bento is only authored if a rule produced it. Cells at assorted sizes is the default mosaic and reads as decoration. Each of these ten has one stated generative rule, and the rule should be legible from the render without reading the label. If it is not, that variant has failed on its own terms.

Cell counts are bound to content, never to what fits: four operating failures, five stages, or whatever the product registry returns. Two variants are built from registry/products.json at runtime so they survive the registry returning a different number of products.

Canon held. No three equal cards, no card nested inside a card, no shadows, one depth cue per cell, charcoal ramp rather than black, and gradient material only ever on a product object with a registry ID. Copy is byte-identical across all ten.

One live core across the whole lab, on the dominant material cell of whichever variant you are looking at. Other material cells carry their exact static twin, so the focal cell is the only thing moving.
