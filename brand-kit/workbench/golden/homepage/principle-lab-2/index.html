One anatomy, five compositions. The models sit above as equal peers, and one wide bar underneath is the business operating layer. That bar carries live AI OS material, because the operating layer is the product and a product element is the only place material belongs. Only one core runs at a time: a single renderer moves to whichever bar is most in view, the same allocation pattern the homepage uses. Every bar keeps its exact static twin behind the canvas, so reduced motion and a missing WebGL context both resolve with no layout shift. Add ?static to the URL to see the still state.
