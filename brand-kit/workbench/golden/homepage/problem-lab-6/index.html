Six variations on V1, not six new ideas. The stack is the champion, so the anatomy stays fixed and two dimensions vary: how the treads step, and what happens where the intelligence block used to be. A drops it for an open edge, B turns it into a labelled line, C changes weight instead of adding anything, D reverses the direction so the layers pile onto you, E adds an accumulating measure, and F is the control with no stepping and no intelligence at all. Still monochrome, still no motion.
