GH-S04 · F iterationsOne composition, ten treatments
12345678910

All ten are F. Hero centred and alive, blocks around it, artefact and caption in every cell. What changes is cell count, hero proportion, card background and where the token sits. Content is identical throughout, so anything you prefer here is a treatment preference and not a content one.

Three of them change the page, not just the cards. 3 puts the cards a step below the page, 4 removes card fills entirely, 5 lets the background carry which blocks have a system. Those are the ones worth looking at hardest, because they change what the section is made of rather than how it is arranged.
