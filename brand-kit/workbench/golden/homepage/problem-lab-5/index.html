Six structural rules, not six pictures. Each one is words, boxes, hairlines and alignment. Nothing is illustrated and nothing moves. All six are monochrome on purpose: using the AI OS material to stand for the generic model layer would say the wrong thing, and holding colour back until Mez appears is a stronger read of the page. Every one is a still, so the champion is where the single live core gets allocated, if it wants one at all.
V1 The stack Rule · distance Every bar between you and the intelligence is a layer you do not own. The argument is carried by the count and the vertical distance, and the bracket in the margin does nothing but count.
